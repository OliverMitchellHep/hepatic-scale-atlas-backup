# Changelog

## v51 — Rex Hepatis: header and dock, Clinical therapeutics, micro views, gallbladder, cirrhosis coupling (29 Sept 2026)
Built on v50 (branch `v48-interface`) from Oli's v50 review.
- **Name.** The project is now **Rex Hepatis**, with the tagline "An interactive 3D model of the hepatobiliary system, from the whole organ down to the individual hepatocyte" beside it in the header (dropped when the header is too narrow). "Macroscopic → subcellular · CT anatomy" is gone. Intro key `hsa-intro-seen-v51`.
- **Header.** Find a structure moves into the header (into the sheet on phones). Panel is replaced by an arrow in the dock's top-right corner that hides the dock; a matching tab on the right edge brings it back. Segments moves to Layers › Views as a "Couinaud segments" toggle (macroscopic scale), its legend still under Views.
- **Dock.** The scale header is just the view title, larger (30 px): the "Scale 0N · ×mag" eyebrow, the size line (e.g. "≈ 400 µm · portal → central") and the descriptive lede are removed at every scale. "The polarised hepatocyte" → "The Hepatocyte".
- **Explore tab removed.** Tabs are Layers | Clinical | Learn (Layers opens first). The Selected card (Isolate, Ghost others, Teaching card) sits at the top of Layers; Key concepts (with Show me) are in Learn as "Key concepts · <scale>", above the modules.
- **Layers at the lobule, acinus and sinusoid.** One flat On | Off list: no expandable structure sub-lists, counts or Ghost column.
- **Clinical.** Collapse all now closes every group and item (then reads Expand all, which reopens the groups). Therapeutics are regrouped: **HPB therapeutics** holds Biliary drainage (Start ERCP first, then the ERCP therapy: Balloon trawl, Plastic stent, Metal stent, Bilateral stents; PTC drain as the percutaneous option; what is in place listed with ×) and Pancreatic duct drainage (its own Start ERCP with Plastic or Metal stent: the cannula and contrast go into the main pancreatic duct and the stent is placed at the final stage). **Liver & portal therapeutics** holds TIPSS, TACE and Resection. The separate ERCP item is gone; ERCP runs show as "ERCP · running" on the item and strip.
- **Views.** Macroscopic home 22 % closer (same direction); the lobule still fades in at the same on-screen size. Lobule → acinus: the micro tiers tip fully (0.45 → 1.02 rad), so the acinus ends straight on with the venules vertical and the plates horizontal, and the camera swings to face it on the way in. Acinus → sinusoid: the sinusoid no longer jumps up and to the left as it arrives (the sinusoid → hepatocyte offset was scaled by 1 − g − s, which jumped by 1 − s at z = 3 once v48's MICRO_K made s ≈ 2); zoom is gentler above the acinus.
- **Gallbladder.** The fundus is closed (its end ring had a 0.2 mm radius and no cap). Its surface vessels come from a new tile (`pipeline/vessels51.py` → `assets/vessels51.webp`, replacing vessels46): one connected network in which new vessels branch from existing ones and vessels that meet join (anastomoses), with one more branching level; drawn at 0.75 tiles per unit (was 0.42) and wrapping a little up the sides. The same tile, with its new connectivity, is used for the arterial vasa vasorum and the portal, venous and biliary surface vessels.
- **Cirrhosis slider (research below).** One stage table drives the slider label, HVPG and liver stiffness together: F0 3 mmHg / 4.5 kPa; F1 3.5–4.5 / 5.5–7.1; F2 4.5–5.5 / 7.1–9.5; F3 5.5–7 / 9.5–12.5; F4 split by Laennec: 4A 7–9 mmHg / 12.5–16 kPa, 4B 9–12.5 / 16–24, 4C 12.5–17 / 24–40. HVPG reaches 10 mmHg at ~18.7 kPa and ≥25 kPa comes with ~13 mmHg; platelets fall below 150 around 11 mmHg (Baveno VII rules agree with the model's HVPG). v50 had HVPG ≈ 8–10 mmHg at F2 and varices at F3 with ~11 kPa.
- **Collaterals.** At 10 mmHg the left gastric vein reverses first (hepatofugal flow up to the cardia; readout "Left gastric vein: reversed"), then the oesophageal/GOV1 varices fill (10 → 16 mmHg); fundal and paraumbilical from 12, splenorenal and caput medusae from 14. A variceal bleed needs a gradient ≥ 12 mmHg with varices. Inserting a TIPSS during a bleed stops it: the gradient falls (17 → 8 mmHg), the left gastric flow returns towards the liver and the varices collapse; the readout notes the bleed stopped. The Portal hypertension module's steps and numbers follow the new coupling.

Research used for the coupling: METAVIR F1–F3 HVPG within the normal range, ≥ 6 mmHg at F3–F4 (HVPG review, Clin Mol Hepatol 2014); Laennec 4A/4B/4C mean HVPG 8.1 / 12.4 / 16.3 mmHg (Kim et al., J Hepatol 2011); VCTE stage cut-offs 7.1 / 9.5 / 12.5 kPa (Castéra et al., Gastroenterology 2005); LSM 13.6 kPa ↔ HVPG ≥ 10 and 17.6 kPa ↔ ≥ 12, r = 0.81, weaker above 10–12 mmHg (Vizzutti et al., Hepatology 2007); Baveno VII rule of five (< 10 kPa excludes cACLD; ≤ 15 kPa with platelets ≥ 150 excludes CSPH; ≥ 25 kPa rules it in); varices form at HVPG ≥ 10 and bleed at ≥ 12; hepatofugal left gastric flow accompanies oesophageal varices (Gastroenterology 1993).

## v49 — flow rules, TIPSS in the vein, micro views, varices, variceal bleed (29 Sept 2026)
- Blood flow: switching the animation off removes the particles (macroscopic and the lobule/acinus/sinusoid flow markers). A closed route (TIPSS removed, collateral closed) takes no new particles and the ones on it fade out within ~0.3 s and re-enter on open routes (before, blood kept running down the old TIPSS path).
- TIPSS: the right hepatic vein is widened about its own centreline to enclose the stent (~0.8 mm clear), tapering where the stent leaves the vein; the HVPG occlusion balloon sits on the vein centreline and inflates to 85% of the vein calibre (it pushed 0.2 mm through the wall).
- Acinus → sinusoid: the zoom converges on one acinar sinusoid and the sinusoid scale opens centred on screen.
- Acinar hepatocytes have the sinusoid scale's cell shape (proportions, rounding, packed edge to edge; 9 per plate instead of 14 thin slabs).
- Acinar bridging fibrosis: straight translucent collagen bands from portal to central venule below the bottom plate and above the top plate (the tubes through the middle of the plates were hidden).
- Varices: the second set of "oesophageal varices" that lay in the gastric lumen are rebuilt as GOV1 gastric varices down the lesser curvature (new registry entry). Oesophageal, gastric and fundal varices lie inside the viscus under the wall; only the feeding veins (left gastric, short gastric) cross the wall at their entry. Varices are dark purple (#46266C), darker than the portal tree.
- New pathology: oesophageal variceal bleed (Portal hypertension → Complication). A lower oesophageal varix ruptures; portal blood is diverted up the left gastric vein and pours into the oesophagus, stomach (part pooling) and duodenum; the gut is ghosted automatically; readout line and Baveno VII management note; registry entry for the rupture point.

## v48 — bile duct colour, falciform, HVPG explainer, micro-tier framing, flow alignment, bile flow (29 Sept 2026)

## v44 — scene, pancreas, falciform, liver material (28 Sept 2026)
- Shadows: real-time shadow maps removed everywhere (renderer, lights, meshes); grounding comes from AO. "Quality: High" now means more AO samples.
- Spine alignment: the macroscopic scene root is rotated so the least-squares centreline through the T1–L5 vertebral body centroids is vertical. Drift from vertical over its 442 mm: before 68 mm (x) / 91 mm (z) (v43 tilted the model 14.4°), after 0.00 / 0.00 mm (`validate.py` spine-axis check, < 2 mm). Default orbit target = spine axis at liver mid-height. `pipeline/spine44.py` derives the constants.
- Percutaneous biopsy view: same viewing angle, zoom removed: distance and target are fitted so the whole liver, right ribs 7–12 and the full needle path fit on screen (allowing for the side panel). The per-frame target-height follow now applies only as the zoom changes, so preset views (biopsy, SVT) keep their target.
- Pancreas remodel (`pipeline/pancreas44.py`): one smooth arc replaces the CT gland's ~90° posterior kink (max bend 2.9°/mm ≈ 20 mm radius, at the neck). Head in the C-loop right of the SMV, uncinate behind the SMV, neck in front of the confluence, body across the aorta at L1–L2 behind the stomach, tail rising to the splenic hilum. Stomach, duodenum, spleen, left kidney, SMV, portal trunk, splenic vein, splenic artery and GDA are subtracted with margins (all clearances > 0). The splenic vein now follows the posterior (tail: posterosuperior) surface; the main duct runs on the new axis. 11k vertices, 0.12 mm max deviation from the implicit surface.
- Pancreas surface blur. Cause: no UVs or maps of its own; the shared 512² procedural atlas was projected triplanar (blend zones average two projections) and its lobulation was a screen-derivative bump of a bilinear-filtered height, 5–20× stronger than other organs', faded out at normal viewing distance by the detail LOD fade, leaving soft blotches; anisotropy capped at 4 (8 on the liver). No mesh LOD switching was involved. Fix: xatlas UVs with seam-welded normals, baked maps from a lobulated high mesh (`pipeline/bake44.py`: tangent normal, self-AO, cavity, curvature, thickness → albedo, ORM with a clearcoat/wetness mask), 2K desktop / 1K phone chosen at load, peach-orange lobules after the maleinternalorans3dsmodel013 reference; anisotropic filtering at the device maximum on every texture.
- Falciform ligament (after the tissue-liver-4 reference): anterior course only, from the dome edge down to the umbilical notch; the wide superior section is removed. A low soft fold (~0.7 mm high on a ~1.2 cm base) in the liver's own red-brown, a step paler, flanks tucked under the capsule (no z-fighting). The ligamentum teres is kept in the scene graph but hidden (`visible=false`, `userData.teachingHidden`).
- Liver bake (`pipeline/bake44.py liver`, headless, reproducible): tangent-space normal, self-AO, cavity, curvature and thickness (plus position) baked from the high mesh; albedo, roughness and a clearcoat/wetness mask composed from them. 4K on desktop, 2K on phones, chosen at load (phones previously had no liver maps). WebP, not KTX2: the artifact host refuses `.ktx2`. `dist` budget 9 → 10 MB for the per-device sets.
- Liver colour: heterogeneous, blending smoothly from a browner superior/diaphragmatic surface (#7A3B2E) to a deeper purple-red inferior/visceral surface (#5A2433), driven by each texel's baked object-space normal plus low-frequency 3D noise through a wide smoothstep (no boundary). Falciform re-matched a step paler than the new palette.
- Liver surface: the lobule pattern is gone (the Worley lobule speckle in the albedo and the lobular close-range detail map, which read as blur at mid distance). New relief after maleinternalorans3dsmodel25 / tissue-liver-5: a slightly coarse capsular grain (~1.3 mm), broad contouring with a few long soft creases (v42's Worley creases made a cellular net once deepened), subcapsular veins faintly raised and darkened. Close range only: `liver44_micro.webp` (fine grain and soft pits, no cells), faded by screen footprint. Wet serosal clearcoat from the baked wetness mask; matte bare area, IVC groove and gallbladder fossa (checked in place). Lighting: environment map + key (world key and viewer headlight); the blue fill and amber rim lights are removed (the rim left gold streaks on the visceral surface), ambient lowered, environment raised.
- Acceptance: the image no longer changes between rotating and at rest (v43 dropped to 1× pixel ratio in motion and faded GTAO in only at rest; GTAO is now off, the baked self-AO grounds the organs, and the Quality toggle is hidden). `validate.py` gains a hard `motion` check (moving frame vs rest frame) and reports rotation frame times. At F4 the falciform fold follows the cirrhotic nodules (it floated or sank before); no seam gaps at F4 (2,660 seam pairs, 0 mm).

## v43 — foundation (27 Sept 2026)
No intended visual change from v42.
- Source moved to this repository: split into `src/` (markup, styles, application, boot), `data/` and `assets/`; `build.py` assembles the page. Built from the published v42 page; the split build was byte-identical to v42 before any change.
- `data/registry.json`: the 255 structure descriptions (previously an inline `INFO` object) with permanent ids, extracted citations and, from the census, the tier and layer each structure lives in. The page's `INFO` is generated from it.
- Render-on-demand: the WebGL render (with AO and shadows) is skipped when nothing visible has changed; animation ticks and the interface keep running.
- `validate.py`: registry, size, load, clickable-name coverage, draw-call budget, idle redraw and screenshot-regression checks, plus warnings for superseded guideline citations.
- `pipeline/`: the v42 liver texture bake scripts (headless Blender).

v42 and earlier: see the project docs `hepatic-scale-atlas-vNN-changes.md`.
