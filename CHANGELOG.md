## v45 — viewer, hepatobiliary geometry, organ materials (28 Sept 2026)
Review screenshots per task in `review/v45/<task>/` (anterior, right lateral, inferior, percutaneous biopsy).
- Default camera: pulled back ×2.04 along the same direction (distance 7.14 → 14.57 units) so the whole ribcage (ribs 1–12, costal cartilages, sternum, T1–T12) fits a 16:9 desktop frame with the side panel open; the orbit target is unchanged.
- Zoom threshold: the lobule now fades in at 17.0× the v44 home framing instead of 6.6× (MACRO_ZOOM 10 → 75.4 at the same MACRO_HOLD 0.82; 34.6× from the new home), i.e. 1.5× the log-zoom travelled before the switch; wheel and pinch steps magnify as much per notch as in v44.
- Pancreatic head: fills the duodenal C-loop and abuts D2's medial wall along its whole height (gap 0.74–1.0 mm; v44 5.6–25 mm). The distal CBD stays inside the head (3–5.6 mm cover) until it meets the duodenal wall at the ampulla with the main duct (v44: it left the gland ~12 mm above the ampulla). IVC, aorta, SMA, left renal vein and right kidney are now subtracted from the gland too.
- Hepatic veins: separate ostia on the anterior/anterolateral suprahepatic IVC just below the caval opening: right hepatic vein anterolateral right (+49°; v44 left the IVC straight laterally, +112°), middle and left hepatic veins through a ~10 mm common trunk anterolateral left (−29°).
- Portal vein: the kink before the bifurcation is gone (41.8 → 4.3 °/mm); bifurcation unchanged.
- Gastroduodenal artery: crosses behind D1, descends on the front of the pancreatic head and divides into the superior pancreaticoduodenal and right gastroepiploic arteries (new, with registry entries); clear of the CBD (0.9 mm) and main duct (4.6 mm) — v44's ran through the duct.
- Falciform ligament: painted into the liver bake (no separate fold mesh): a 10 mm pale cream fibrous band from the umbilical notch, flaring at the superior margin into a fan whose layers diverge into the coronary ligament across the top (new registry entry); triangular ligaments not modelled; ligamentum teres hidden.
- Gallbladder fossa: v44's bed was a pleated imprint of the gallbladder tube (146 folded triangles; the gallbladder poked 0.1 mm into the liver). Re-meshed as a concave bowl (9.5 mm deep, ≥ 1.2 mm clear of the gallbladder, 5 sub-mm² slivers left at the fundus notch), re-unwrapped and textured (areolar bed) in the liver bake.
- Spleen: moved 0.58 mm to clear the stomach by 1.1 mm (it touched at 0.53 mm). A closer fit to the fundus is not reachable with its shape unchanged (rigid search ±16°/±6 mm: best median gap 4.4 mm vs 3.7 mm now).
- Liver material: superior #5E2A1F, inferior #431628 (v44 #7A3B2E / #5A2433); the transition sits 0.25 higher in normal-y with the same blend width; soft ~7 mm surface nodularity (flat domes, narrow soft creases) with a finer grain reduced against shimmer; falciform edges anti-aliased; page reference mean albedo #4E2024 (tissue only).
- Organ materials: the scripted liver bake (`pipeline/bake44.py`: tangent/object normal, self-AO, cavity, curvature, thickness → albedo, roughness, clearcoat/wetness) now covers the stomach, duodenum, oesophagus, spleen, pancreas and both kidneys, each at 4K desktop / 2K phone (WebP; the host refuses KTX2). `pipeline/organs45.py`: subtle serosal irregularity throughout; surface vessels grown on each organ's surface (dark red core, pale red edges, slightly raised, branching) — stomach: gastroepiploic arcade along the greater curvature and left/right gastric arcade along the lesser curvature painted on the gastric surface (no omentum), with serosal branches across both walls; duodenum: pancreaticoduodenal arcade on the medial border with branches; oesophagus: longitudinal trunks and branches over longitudinal adventitial fibres; spleen: smooth capsule with notches on the superior border; pancreas: lobulated (4K now); kidneys: capsule with faint capsular vessels and a hint of perinephric fat. UV charts have 32 px gutters (mip-safe); per-vertex tangents; lossless albedo on the smooth spleen and kidney capsules. `dist` budget 10 → 26 MB; the test harness waits for all organ maps. Unresolved: a faint blocky pattern in the spleen's close-range highlight (cause not isolated).

## v44 — scene, pancreas, falciform, liver material (28 Sept 2026)
- Shadows: real-time shadow maps removed everywhere (renderer, lights, meshes); grounding comes from AO. "Quality: High" now means more AO samples.
- Spine alignment: the macroscopic scene root is rotated so the least-squares centreline through the T1–L5 vertebral body centroids is vertical. Drift from vertical over its 442 mm: before 68 mm (x) / 91 mm (z) (v43 tilted the model 14.4°), after 0.00 / 0.00 mm (`validate.py` spine-axis check, < 2 mm). Default orbit target = spine axis at liver mid-height. `pipeline/spine44.py` derives the constants.
- Percutaneous biopsy view: same viewing angle, zoom removed: distance and target are fitted so the whole liver, right ribs 7–12 and the full needle path fit on screen (allowing for the side panel). The per-frame target-height follow now applies only as the zoom changes, so preset views (biopsy, SVT) keep their target.
- Pancreas remodel (`pipeline/pancreas44.py`): one smooth arc replaces the CT gland's ~90° posterior kink (max bend 2.9°/mm ≈ 20 mm radius, at the neck). Head in the C-loop right of the SMV, uncinate behind the SMV, neck in front of the confluence, body across the aorta at L1–L2 behind the stomach, tail rising to the splenic hilum. Stomach, duodenum, spleen, left kidney, SMV, portal trunk, splenic vein, splenic artery and GDA are subtracted with margins (all clearances > 0). The splenic vein now follows the posterior (tail: posterosuperior) surface; the main duct runs on the new axis. 11k vertices, 0.12 mm max deviation from the implicit surface.
- Pancreas surface blur. Cause: no UVs or maps of its own; the shared 512² procedural atlas was projected triplanar (blend zones average two projections) and its lobulation was a screen-derivative bump of a bilinear-filtered height, 5–20× stronger than other organs', faded out at normal viewing distance by the detail LOD fade, leaving soft blotches; anisotropy capped at 4 (8 on the liver). No mesh LOD switching was involved. Fix: xatlas UVs with seam-welded normals, baked maps from a lobulated high mesh (`pipeline/bake44.py`: tangent normal, self-AO, cavity, curvature, thickness → albedo, ORM with a clearcoat/wetness mask), 2K desktop / 1K phone chosen at load, peach-orange lobules after the maleinternalorans3dsmodel013 reference; anisotropic filtering at the device maximum on every texture.
- Falciform ligament (after the tissue-liver-4 reference): anterior course only, from the dome edge down to the umbilical notch; the wide superior section is removed. A low soft fold (~0.7 mm high on a ~1.2 cm base) in the liver's own red-brown, a step paler, flanks tucked under the capsule (no z-fighting). The ligamentum teres is kept in the scene graph but hidden (`visible=false`, `userData.teachingHidden`).
- Liver bake (`pipeline/bake44.py liver`, headless, reproducible): tangent-space normal, self-AO, cavity, curvature and thickness (plus position) baked from the high mesh; albedo, roughness and a clearcoat/wetness mask composed from them. 4K on desktop, 2K on phones, chosen at load (phones previously had no liver maps). WebP, not KTX2: the artifact host refuses `.ktx2`. `dist` budget 9 → 10 MB for the per-device sets.
- Liver colour: heterogeneous, blending smoothly from a browner superior/diaphragmatic surface (#7A3B2E) to a deeper purple-red inferior/visceral surface (#5A2433), driven by each texel's baked object-space normal plus low-frequency 3D noise through a wide smoothstep (no boundary). Falciform re-matched a step paler than the new palette.
- Liver surface: the lobule pattern is gone (the Worley lobule speckle in the albedo and the lobular close-range detail map, which read as blur at mid distance). New relief after maleinternalorans3dsmodel25 / tissue-liver-5: a slightly coarse capsular grain (~1.3 mm), broad contouring with a few long soft creases (v42's Worley creases made a cellular net once deepened), subcapsular veins faintly raised and darkened. Close range only: `liver44_micro.webp` (fine grain and soft pits, no cells), faded by screen footprint. Wet serosal clearcoat from the baked wetness mask; matte bare area, IVC groove and gallbladder fossa (checked in place). Lighting: environment map + key (world key and viewer headlight); the blue fill and amber rim lights are removed (the rim left gold streaks on the visceral surface), ambient lowered, environment raised.
- Acceptance: the image no longer changes between rotating and at rest (v43 dropped to 1× pixel ratio in motion and faded GTAO in only at rest; GTAO is now off, the baked self-AO grounds the organs, and the Quality toggle is hidden). `validate.py` gains a hard `motion` check (moving frame vs rest frame) and reports rotation frame times. At F4 the falciform fold follows the cirrhotic nodules (it floated or sank before); no seam gaps at F4 (2,660 seam pairs, 0 mm).
- Review screenshots per task in `review/<task>/` (`tests/review.py`); `review/0` is v43.

## v43 — foundation (27 Sept 2026)
No intended visual change from v42.
- Source moved to this repository: split into `src/` (markup, styles, application, boot), `data/` and `assets/`; `build.py` assembles the page. Built from the published v42 page; the split build was byte-identical to v42 before any change.
- `data/registry.json`: the 255 structure descriptions (previously an inline `INFO` object) with permanent ids, extracted citations and, from the census, the tier and layer each structure lives in. The page's `INFO` is generated from it.
- Render-on-demand: the WebGL render (with AO and shadows) is skipped when nothing visible has changed; animation ticks and the interface keep running.
- `validate.py`: registry, size, load, clickable-name coverage, draw-call budget, idle redraw and screenshot-regression checks, plus warnings for superseded guideline citations.
- `pipeline/`: the v42 liver texture bake scripts (headless Blender).

v42 and earlier: see the project docs `hepatic-scale-atlas-vNN-changes.md`.
