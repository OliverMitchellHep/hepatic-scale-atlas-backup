# P06 · Synthetic function, coagulation and albumin

**Level:** MRCP  **Prerequisites:** P02  **Draft:** 29 Sept 2026

Evidence tags: **[V]** checked against a source found by web search; **[T]** standard textbook physiology, not opened this session; **[UNVERIFIED]** could not be confirmed. The coagulation cascade is a non-spatial diagram: [NEEDS ASSET: a 2D diagram block].

## Learning outcomes
1. List the main proteins made by the liver and the clinical markers of synthetic function.
2. Explain why INR is an acute marker of synthetic function and why it does not measure bleeding risk in cirrhosis.
3. Describe rebalanced haemostasis in liver disease and its consequences.
4. Explain albumin's roles and the evidence for albumin therapy in decompensated cirrhosis.

## 1 · What the liver makes

### 1.1 Products
- Albumin, most clotting factors (I, II, V, VII, IX, X, XI, XII, XIII), antithrombin, protein C and S, transport proteins, complement components, acute-phase proteins and hormone-binding globulins. [T]
- Factor VIII is made largely by endothelial cells including LSECs, not hepatocytes, and is often raised in cirrhosis. [T; **[UNVERIFIED]**]

### 1.2 Markers
- Short half-lives make **prothrombin time/INR** (factor VII, hours) responsive in acute injury and **albumin** (weeks) a marker of chronic dysfunction. [T]

## 2 · Coagulation in liver disease

**Atlas view.** Diagram [NEEDS ASSET]. Atlas structures for context: `portal-vein-thrombus-acute-occlusive`, `smv-thrombus`.

### 2.1 Rebalanced haemostasis
- In liver disease, changes in both pro- and anti-haemostatic pathways can leave haemostasis balanced; INR reflects only procoagulant factor deficiency and not the reduced anticoagulant proteins. [V: Lisman & Porte 2010, review]
- Patients with cirrhosis are not "auto-anticoagulated" and may be in a procoagulant state, with resistance to anticoagulation and an increased risk of venous thromboembolism. [V: Tripodi & Mannucci 2011, NEJM review]

### 2.2 Consequences
- INR should not be used to gate invasive procedures or to predict bleeding in stable cirrhosis, and correcting it with plasma is not supported. [V: Tripodi & Mannucci 2011 raise this as the question; recommendation wording and current guidance **[UNVERIFIED]**, check AGA/BSG guidance]

## 3 · Albumin

### 3.1 Roles
- Oncotic pressure, and binding and transport of bilirubin, fatty acids, drugs and hormones. [T]
- In decompensated cirrhosis albumin has additional immunomodulatory and antioxidant functions and is altered structurally. [V: Bernardi 2020, review]

### 3.2 Clinical use
- The ANSWER open-label randomised trial reported that long-term albumin plus standard medical treatment improved 18-month survival (77% vs 66%; HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.40–0.95) in patients with uncomplicated ascites. [V: Caraceni 2018, single open-label trial; results of other trials were not checked, so **[UNVERIFIED]**: do not teach this as settled]

## 4 · Clinical correlation

- **INR in cirrhosis:** an INR of 1.5 in cirrhosis does not by itself indicate bleeding risk; variceal bleeding is portal-pressure-driven. [V: rebalanced haemostasis; T for bleeding]
- **Thrombosis:** portal vein thrombosis and VTE occur despite a raised INR. [V: Tripodi & Mannucci 2011]
- **SBP and paracentesis:** albumin is given with large-volume paracentesis and in SBP per EASL 2018 guidance (over 5 years old). [T; **[UNVERIFIED]**, check the guideline text]

## 5 · Self-assessment (SBA)

**Q1.** Which protein's short half-life makes INR a marker of acute synthetic function?
A. Albumin  B. Factor VII  C. Fibrinogen  D. Ferritin  E. Transferrin
**Answer: B.** Factor VII has the shortest half-life of the vitamin K–dependent factors.

**Q2.** In stable cirrhosis with an INR of 1.6, which is correct?
A. The patient is auto-anticoagulated  B. Haemostasis may be rebalanced and thrombosis can occur  C. FFP should always be given before biopsy  D. Vitamin K will normalise the INR  E. INR predicts variceal bleeding
**Answer: B.**

**Q3.** Which factor is largely not made by hepatocytes?
A. Factor VII  B. Fibrinogen  C. Factor VIII  D. Factor IX  E. Antithrombin
**Answer: C.** Factor VIII is made largely by endothelial cells. **[T; UNVERIFIED]**

**Q4.** Which statement about the ANSWER trial is best supported?
A. It was blinded and placebo-controlled  B. Long-term albumin improved 18-month survival in an open-label design  C. It showed no benefit  D. It enrolled only patients with SBP  E. It has been confirmed by every other trial
**Answer: B.** It was open-label; other trials were not checked.

**Q5.** Which is the best marker of chronic synthetic dysfunction?
A. INR  B. Albumin  C. ALT  D. ALP  E. GGT
**Answer: B.** Albumin has a half-life of weeks.
