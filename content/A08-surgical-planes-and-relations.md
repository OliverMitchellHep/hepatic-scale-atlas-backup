# A08 · Surgical planes, relations and resection

**Level:** MRCP → registrar  **Prerequisites:** A02, A03, A04  **Draft:** 29 Sept 2026

Evidence tags: **[V]** checked against a source found by web search; **[T]** standard textbook anatomy, not opened this session; **[UNVERIFIED]** could not be confirmed. Segment boundaries and terminology are in A02; the HVPG and Child–Pugh resection criteria are in A02 section 6.3 and are not repeated.

## Learning outcomes
1. Name the standard hepatectomies by segments resected, in Brisbane/Tokyo terms.
2. Explain inflow and outflow control and the manoeuvres that provide them.
3. State the relations that limit mobilisation of the right and left liver.
4. Define the future liver remnant, the 20/30/40% rule and why it is only a guide, and describe portal vein embolisation.
5. Define post-hepatectomy liver failure and its ISGLS grades.

### 1.1 Brisbane terms
- Hemihepatectomy (right V–VIII; left II–IV, each with or without I), sectionectomy (second order, for example left lateral II–III), segmentectomy (third order). Extended hemihepatectomy and trisectionectomy add adjacent segments. [V: hemiliver/section/segment order from Strasberg 2000; segment lists T]
- Tokyo 2020 updates Brisbane with additional definitions. [V: Wakabayashi 2022, existence and purpose]

## 2 · Inflow, outflow and mobilisation

**Atlas view.** Tier 0, anterior and posterior. `portal` On, `artery` On, `hepaticVein` On, `ivc` On, `diaphragm` Ghost.
Buttons: **"Hepatoduodenal ligament (inflow)"** (highlight `portal-trunk`, `proper-hepatic-artery`, `common-bile-duct`) · **"Hepatic vein ostia (outflow)"** (`right-hepatic-vein`, `middle-and-left-hepatic-vein-trunk`, `ivc`) · **"Bare area"** [NEEDS ASSET].

### 2.1 Inflow control
- The **Pringle manoeuvre** occludes the hepatic artery and portal vein by compressing or clamping the hepatoduodenal ligament. [V: Pringle 1908; the series described eight trauma patients, all of whom died]
- Selective hemihepatic inflow control uses the right or left pedicle. [T]

### 2.2 Outflow and the liver-hanging manoeuvre
- Outflow control refers to clamping the hepatic veins or suprahepatic and infrahepatic IVC. [T]
- The **liver-hanging manoeuvre** (Belghiti) lifts the liver during transection by a tape passed between the IVC and the liver, and was described to guide the direction of anatomical transection during right hepatectomy without full mobilisation. [V: Belghiti 2001]

### 2.3 Relations that limit mobilisation
- Right: the bare area and coronary ligament, the right adrenal and the IVC (short hepatic veins). Left: the left triangular ligament, the gastro-oesophageal junction and the spleen. [T]

## 3 · Future liver remnant

**Atlas view.** Tier 0. Resection simulation. The atlas reports the remnant fraction for each planned segmental resection.
Button: **"Show remnant volume"** (`transection-surface-remnant`).

### 3.1 The rule
- The proposed 20/30/40% rule sets minimum future liver remnant fractions of about 20% in a normal liver, 30% after chemotherapy and 40% in cirrhosis or after long chemotherapy. Published thresholds vary between studies and methods and do not always fit the rule. [V: search summary of reviews; Vauthey 2000 and Kishi 2009 as key sources, exact figures not read]
- FLR is expressed as a fraction of the standardised total liver volume, not of the imaged liver. [V: Vauthey 2000 title; detail T]

### 3.2 Portal vein embolisation
- Embolising the portal branch to the liver to be removed diverts flow to the remnant and induces hypertrophy. [T; results and criteria: **[UNVERIFIED]**, no source verified here]
- A European survey of FLR volumetry practice was published in 2025 (E-AHPBA). [V: existence only]

## 4 · Post-hepatectomy liver failure (PHLF)

### 4.1 Definition
- The ISGLS defines PHLF as impaired ability of the liver to maintain synthetic, excretory and detoxifying function, with raised INR and hyperbilirubinaemia on or after postoperative day 5. [V: Rahbari 2011]
- **Grade A** needs no change in management; **B** deviates from the regular course without invasive therapy; **C** needs invasive treatment. [V: Rahbari 2011]

## 5 · Self-assessment (SBA)

**Q1.** In Brisbane 2000 terms, resection of the right anterior section (V and VIII) is a:
A. Hemihepatectomy  B. Sectionectomy  C. Segmentectomy  D. Trisectionectomy  E. Wedge resection
**Answer: B.** Second-order division is the section; the right anterior section contains V and VIII.

**Q2.** Which manoeuvre occludes both hepatic arterial and portal inflow?
A. Liver hanging  B. Pringle  C. Kocher  D. Cattell–Braasch  E. Hanging of the IVC
**Answer: B.** The Pringle manoeuvre compresses the hepatoduodenal ligament.

**Q3.** The liver-hanging manoeuvre is described mainly to:
A. Control the hepatic artery  B. Guide anatomical transection and allow right hepatectomy without full mobilisation  C. Ligate the left portal vein  D. Occlude the IVC  E. Drain the gallbladder
**Answer: B.** A tape passed between IVC and liver lifts the liver and guides the transection plane.

**Q4.** The proposed minimum future liver remnant in a patient with cirrhosis is about:
A. 10%  B. 20%  C. 30%  D. 40%  E. 60%
**Answer: D.** The proposed rule is 20% (normal), 30% (chemotherapy) and 40% (cirrhosis), but thresholds vary between studies.

**Q5.** Which finding on postoperative day 5 fits ISGLS PHLF?
A. Bilirubin normal, INR normal  B. Raised INR and hyperbilirubinaemia  C. Raised ALT alone  D. Low haemoglobin  E. Fever
**Answer: B.** PHLF is defined by raised INR and hyperbilirubinaemia on or after day 5.
