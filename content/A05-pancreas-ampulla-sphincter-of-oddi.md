# A05 · Pancreas, ampulla and sphincter of Oddi

**Level:** MRCP  **Prerequisites:** A03, A04  **Draft:** 29 Sept 2026

Evidence tags: **[V]** checked against a source found by web search; **[T]** standard textbook anatomy, not opened this session; **[UNVERIFIED]** could not be confirmed.

## Learning outcomes
1. Describe the parts of the pancreas, its ductal system and its relations to the SMV, SMA, splenic vein, CBD and duodenum.
2. Explain the anatomy of the ampulla and the components of the sphincter of Oddi, and the pancreaticobiliary junction.
3. Describe pancreas divisum, including its prevalence and why it matters.
4. Apply the anatomy to ERCP cannulation, sphincterotomy and post-ERCP pancreatitis.

## 1 · Pancreas: parts and relations

**Atlas view.** Tier 0, anterior. `pancreas` On, `duodenum` On, `portal` On, `artery` On, `stomach` Ghost, `parenchyma` Ghost.
Buttons: **"Head in the duodenal C-loop"** (highlight `pancreas`, `duodenum`, `common-bile-duct`) · **"Neck and confluence"** (`splenic-vein`, `superior-mesenteric-vein`, `portal-trunk`) · **"Body and tail to the splenic hilum"** (`splenic-artery`, `spleen`).

### 1.1 Parts
- **Head** (in the C-loop of the duodenum, with the uncinate process behind the SMV), **neck** (in front of the portal–SMV confluence), **body** (across the aorta at about L1–L2, behind the stomach) and **tail** (towards the splenic hilum). [T; the atlas models these relations, v44/v46]
- The atlas pancreatic head is a single rounded mass in the C-loop; the CBD enters from above and stays inside the head to the ampulla. [Atlas note, v46]

### 1.2 Relations
- Posteriorly: the CBD, IVC, aorta, left renal vein, SMA; the splenic vein runs along the posterior surface of the body and tail. [T]
- The gastroduodenal artery runs on the anterior head; the head is supplied by the pancreaticoduodenal arcades (A03). [T]

## 2 · Ductal system

**Atlas view.** Tier 0, close to the pancreatic head. `pancreas` Ghost, `biliary` On.
Buttons: **"Main pancreatic duct"** (highlight `pancreatic-duct`) · **"Ampulla of Vater"** (`ampulla-of-vater`, `common-bile-duct`). Accessory duct and minor papilla: [NEEDS ASSET].

### 2.1 Main and accessory ducts
- The **main duct (Wirsung)** runs along the gland from tail to head and usually joins the CBD at the ampulla. The **accessory duct (Santorini)** drains the upper head into the duodenum at the minor papilla, above the major papilla. [T]
- Embryology: the dorsal and ventral pancreatic buds fuse; the ventral duct normally becomes the proximal main duct. [T]

### 2.2 Pancreas divisum
- Failure of the dorsal and ventral ducts to fuse leaves most of the gland draining through the accessory duct and minor papilla. [T]
- Reported prevalence depends on method and population. A recent meta-analysis reports a pooled global prevalence of 11.1%: 18.7% in people with pancreatic disease, 8.8% in cadaveric studies, 5.6% in healthy individuals and 4.7% in consecutive patients. [V: Medicina 2026 meta-analysis, figures from search summary; authors not confirmed, so **[UNVERIFIED citation]**]
- Older reviews cite about 7% at autopsy (range 1–14%), 3–8% at ERCP and 9% at MRCP; the same secondary source reports divisum in 50% of idiopathic pancreatitis versus 4% of controls in a series. [V: secondary summary, **[UNVERIFIED]**]

## 3 · Ampulla and sphincter of Oddi

### 3.1 Anatomy
- The CBD and main pancreatic duct usually meet in a common channel in the duodenal wall and open at the major papilla. The muscle around the terminal CBD, the terminal pancreatic duct and the common channel is the **sphincter of Oddi**, which has separate biliary, pancreatic and ampullary parts. [T; no modelled sub-structure, [NEEDS ASSET]]
- Common channel length varies; a long common channel is associated with reflux of pancreatic juice into the biliary tree. [T; **[UNVERIFIED]**]

### 3.2 Function
- The sphincter regulates flow of bile and pancreatic juice into the duodenum and prevents duodenal reflux; it relaxes in response to CCK and during the fed state (P03). [T]

### 3.3 Sphincter of Oddi dysfunction
- The Rome IV working team (Cotton 2016) recognises functional biliary and pancreatic sphincter of Oddi disorders and notes that results of commonly performed treatments (cholecystectomy, sphincterotomy) are not uniformly good. [V: Cotton 2016; over 5 years old, check for update]

## 4 · Clinical correlation

### 4.1 Gallstone pancreatitis and the common channel
- A stone impacted at or passing through the ampulla can obstruct the pancreatic duct and cause pancreatitis; the shared channel is the anatomical basis. [T]

### 4.2 ERCP: cannulation and sphincterotomy
- The bile duct axis runs up and to the right from the papilla (about 11 to 1 o'clock on the endoscopic view); the biliary sphincterotomy is cut in that direction. [T; **[UNVERIFIED]**, standard endoscopy teaching]
- The atlas models the duodenoscope, cannula and sphincterotome, biliary sphincterotomy and extraction balloon, and plastic/metal stents. [Atlas note]

### 4.3 Post-ERCP pancreatitis
- ESGE (2020) recommends 100 mg rectal diclofenac or indomethacin immediately before ERCP in all patients without contraindication, and prophylactic pancreatic stenting in selected high-risk patients (inadvertent guidewire passage or opacification of the pancreatic duct, double-guidewire cannulation). [V: Dumonceau 2020; guideline is over 5 years old, check for update; grade **[UNVERIFIED]**]
- Pancreas divisum and sphincter of Oddi dysfunction are recognised risk situations for ERCP. [T; **[UNVERIFIED]**]

## 5 · Self-assessment (SBA)

**Q1.** Which structure lies immediately behind the neck of the pancreas?
A. IVC  B. Portal–SMV confluence  C. Left renal vein  D. Aorta  E. Coeliac trunk
**Answer: B.** The neck lies in front of the confluence of the SMV and splenic vein. The uncinate process passes behind the SMV.

**Q2.** Which duct drains the upper head through the minor papilla?
A. Main duct  B. Accessory duct (Santorini)  C. Cystic duct  D. CBD  E. Duct of Luschka
**Answer: B.**

**Q3.** In pancreas divisum:
A. The ventral duct drains most of the gland  B. Most of the gland drains through the minor papilla  C. The CBD opens into the stomach  D. Sphincterotomy is contraindicated  E. The pancreatic duct is absent
**Answer: B.** Failure of ductal fusion leaves the dorsal duct draining the bulk of the gland.

**Q4.** In the meta-analysis quoted, the pooled prevalence of pancreas divisum was about:
A. 1%  B. 5%  C. 11%  D. 25%  E. 50%
**Answer: C.** Pooled prevalence 11.1%, but lower in healthy individuals (5.6%) and consecutive patients (4.7%). **[UNVERIFIED citation; check before publishing.]**

**Q5.** ESGE 2020 guidance recommends which prophylaxis before ERCP in all patients without contraindication?
A. Prophylactic antibiotics  B. Rectal NSAID (diclofenac or indomethacin 100 mg)  C. Hydration alone  D. Octreotide  E. Glyceryl trinitrate
**Answer: B.** Guideline is over 5 years old; check for an update before using in teaching.
