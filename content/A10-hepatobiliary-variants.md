# A10 · Anatomical variants of the hepatobiliary system

**Level:** MRCP → registrar  **Prerequisites:** A03, A04, A05  **Draft:** 29 Sept 2026

Evidence tags: **[V]** checked against a source found by web search; **[T]** standard textbook anatomy, not opened this session; **[UNVERIFIED]** could not be confirmed. This module is a classification key. The prevalence figures, and their sources, are quoted from the module where each was introduced (A02, A04, A05) so that no number is defined twice.

## Learning outcomes
1. Use the Michels/Hiatt, Covey, Huang/Choi and Todani classifications correctly.
2. State each variant's published prevalence with its source, and say how the population and method affect the figure.
3. Identify which variants change the operative or interventional plan.

## 1 · Which variants change management

**Atlas view.** The atlas models one CT anatomy; variants need a library. [NEEDS ASSET: alternative meshes or overlays for replaced right hepatic artery, portal trifurcation, right posterior duct anomalies, pancreas divisum]. The choledochal cyst types are in the registry: `choledochal-cyst-todani-i` to `-v`, `choledochal-cyst`.

| Domain | Variant | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Arterial | Replaced/accessory right hepatic artery from the SMA | Preserve during pancreatoduodenectomy and right hepatectomy; map before TACE/SIRT |
| Portal | Trifurcation; right posterior branch first | Right portal ligation/embolisation, right hepatectomy |
| Biliary | Right posterior duct into the left duct or CHD; triple confluence; accessory duct | Hilar resection, living donor division, bile duct injury |
| Pancreatic | Divisum | ERCP cannulation; recurrent pancreatitis |
| Cystic | Choledochal cyst | Malignancy risk, surgery |

## 2 · Arterial: Michels and Hiatt

- **Michels (1966):** ten basic patterns of hepatic supply and 26 collateral pathways, from 200 dissections. [V: Michels 1966]
- **Hiatt (1994):** six types in 1,000 liver donors; Type 1 standard in about 76% and Type 3 (replaced or accessory right hepatic artery from the SMA) in 10.6%. [V: Hiatt 1994, percentages from search summary]
- Prevalence of other Hiatt types, and of the left-sided variants, is not given here. [UNVERIFIED]

## 3 · Portal: Covey

- Series of 200 patients: standard 65%, trifurcation 9%, right posterior portal vein as first branch 13%. [V: Covey 2004, percentages from search summary]

## 4 · Biliary: Huang and Choi

- **Huang** classifies the right posterior sectoral duct insertion into types A1–A5 for donor evaluation. [V: Huang 1996, described in later series]
- The frequency of each type differs by cohort: one series gives A1 50%, A4 20.3%, A2 and A3 14.1% each; another A1 65.8%, A2 11.7%, A3 13.3%, A4 7.5%, A5 1.7%. [V: secondary summary of two cohorts; sources not identified, **[UNVERIFIED citations]**]
- **Choi 2003** (300 donors, intraoperative cholangiography): typical anatomy 63%; right posterior duct into the left duct 11%; triple confluence 10%; right posterior duct into the CHD 6%; into the cystic duct 2%; accessory duct 5%. [V: Choi 2003]
- **Mortelé and Ros (2001):** right posterior duct into the left duct 13–19%; accessory ducts about 2%. [V: pictorial essay]

## 5 · Pancreatic

- Pancreas divisum: pooled prevalence 11.1%, with 18.7% in pancreatic disease, 8.8% in cadaveric series, 5.6% in healthy people and 4.7% in consecutive patients. [V: Medicina 2026 meta-analysis, **[UNVERIFIED citation]**; A05]

## 6 · Choledochal cysts

- **Todani (1977)** classification: five types based on location and extent of biliary dilatation; the registry holds `choledochal-cyst-todani-i` to `-v` (including IVa and IVb). [V: Todani 1977, existence and purpose]
- Cancer arising in the cyst is part of the paper's title, so malignancy risk is the reason for resection. [V: title only]

## 7 · Hepatic venous

- Nakamura and Tsuzuki described the ramification patterns and variants of the hepatic veins in 83 autopsies; inferior right hepatic veins are frequent and may be large. [V: Nakamura & Tsuzuki 1981, prevalence figure **[UNVERIFIED]**]

## 8 · Clinical correlation

- **Living donor and split-liver planning:** donor selection and technique depend on the bile duct anatomy and the arterial/portal anatomy. [V: Huang classification described as important for donor selection; T]
- **TACE and SIRT:** variant hepatic arterial anatomy is common, reported in up to 45% of patients, and mapping is needed to avoid non-target delivery. [V: review summary; **[UNVERIFIED citation]**]
- **Bile duct injury:** aberrant right posterior duct anatomy increases risk (A04). [T]

## 9 · Self-assessment (SBA)

**Q1.** In Hiatt's donor series, which finding was present in 10.6%?
A. Replaced left hepatic artery from the left gastric  B. Replaced or accessory right hepatic artery from the SMA  C. Coeliac trunk absence  D. Double replaced hepatic arteries  E. Trifurcation of the portal vein
**Answer: B.**

**Q2.** In Covey's series of 200 patients, the right posterior portal vein arose as the first branch of the main portal vein in about:
A. 1%  B. 5%  C. 13%  D. 35%  E. 65%
**Answer: C.** Trifurcation was 9%; standard anatomy was 65%.

**Q3.** According to Choi 2003, the most common intrahepatic biliary variant in living donors was:
A. Triple confluence  B. Right posterior duct into the left duct  C. Right posterior duct into the cystic duct  D. Accessory duct  E. Absent right duct
**Answer: B.** 11%, versus 10% for triple confluence. Typical anatomy: 63%.

**Q4.** Which classification describes choledochal cysts?
A. Michels  B. Todani  C. Bismuth–Corlette  D. Sarin  E. Huang
**Answer: B.** Bismuth–Corlette is for hilar cholangiocarcinoma, Sarin for gastric varices, Huang for right intrahepatic ducts, Michels for hepatic arteries.

**Q5.** Which is the most important reason to know the right posterior duct's insertion before a donor right hepatectomy?
A. To time the operation  B. To decide how many duct anastomoses the graft will need  C. To assess portal flow  D. To measure the IVC  E. To plan the incision
**Answer: B.** Anomalous insertion determines whether the graft has one or more duct orifices. **[T]**
