Rex Hepatis is a teaching resource for medical students through to gastroenterology and hepatology registrars. It is an educational model and is not for clinical decision-making.

The 3D atlas is derived from a single segmented CT case; see the atlas information panels for sources.

## Credits and licences

**CT anatomy.** The liver and neighbouring organ surfaces, ribs, spine and diaphragm are derived from case s1267 of the TotalSegmentator dataset (adult, portal-venous phase), licensed [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Wasserthal J, Breit HC, Meyer MT, et al. TotalSegmentator: robust segmentation of 104 anatomic structures in CT images. *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence* 2023;5(5):e230024. doi:[10.1148/ryai.230024](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.230024). The surfaces here are adapted from the dataset's segmentation masks: the meshes are smoothed, registered and textured, the left lobe is shortened, and the vessels, bile ducts and gallbladder are modelled and fitted to the anatomy. The original scan and masks are not distributed here, and the dataset's authors do not endorse this site.

**Software.** The atlas uses [three.js](https://threejs.org/) (MIT).

Rex Hepatis is illustrative and not for clinical decisions. Guideline sources: EASL, BSG, UEG, NICE, NHS/NHSBT, Baveno, ESGE.
